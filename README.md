## 🚀 Featured Bioinformatics Projects

| Project | Domain | Highlights | Stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[RiboFlow](https://github.com/emiresti/riboflow)** | Translation Kinetics & Synthetic Biology | Exact continuous-time **$\ell$-TASEP ribosome traffic simulator**, **disome collision / NGD risk analyzer**, **synonymous ("silent") mutation hotspot scanner**, and **pause-preserving codon harmonizer** with live HTML5 Canvas lab & CLI. | `Python` `HTML5 Canvas` `JavaScript` |
| **[ProteinScope](https://github.com/emiresti/proteinscope)** *(Live Demo)* | Structural Bioinformatics | Browser-based **3D protein viewer** powered by 3Dmol.js, RCSB PDB & UniProt APIs with from-scratch MW, isoelectric point ($pI$), GRAVY hydropathy, and net-charge calculators. | `JavaScript` `3Dmol.js` `WebGL` |
| **[Cell-Morphology-Analyzer](https://github.com/emiresti/Cell-Morphology-Analyzer)** | Bioimage Analysis | Multi-channel **fluorescence (GFP, DAPI, RFP)** & brightfield microscopy segmentation with distance-transform cell separation, circularity/solidity morphometrics, SQLite history, and CSV/PDF lab reports. | `Python` `OpenCV` `Streamlit` `SQLite` |
| **[genom-analiz-araci](https://github.com/emiresti/genom-analiz-araci)** | Sequence & Cloning Genomics | **NCBI Entrez** fetcher, sliding-window GC/$T_m$ analyzer, **6-frame ORF finder**, and **14-enzyme restriction digest simulator** (linear DNA & circular plasmids) with Streamlit UI & CLI. | `Python` `Biopython` `Streamlit` |
